## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the sensory luxury, obsessive craftsmanship, and warmth of an artisanal confectionery and haute pâtisserie atelier. The experience channels the sensorial indulgence of melted cocoa, velvet praline, and warm spun sugar into a clean, modern digital space.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Tactile Warm Minimalism:** Generous warm milk space, refined typographic scale, and soft organic shapes replace clinical coldness with tactile indulgence.
- **Epicurean Richness:** Surfaces evoke velvety ganache, crisp puff pastry layers, and buttery textures through carefully controlled tonal transitions.
- **Sensory Refinement:** High-contrast editorial display typography juxtaposed with ultra-legible modern functional copy creates an authoritative, bespoke lifestyle atmosphere.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the confectionery atelier: single-origin dark cacao, slow-cooked caramel, toasted almond flour, and fresh whipped cream.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#2A160E` - Roasted Cacao):** Anchors high-hierarchy headings, high-intent call-to-actions, and structural brand anchors. Deep variant `#1F1009` serves as active/pressed states.
- **Secondary (`#C87A38` - Burnished Caramel):** Applied to active indicators, primary highlights, cart indicators, and focused states. Lighter tint `#E29548` is used for dynamic hover interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#B87333` - Spun Copper / Gold):** Reserved for badges of excellence, bespoke packaging tiers, seasonal flags, and delicate metallic accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FDFBF7` / `#F8F4EC` - Warm Milk / Clotted Cream):** Primary page background and card fill foundations, banishing harsh `#FFFFFF` blues for natural, culinary warmth.
- **Neutral Container (`#EFE7D8` - Sweet Almond):** Subdued surface container for secondary card wells, input backgrounds, structural dividers, and subtle tag fills.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes classical European high-fashion editorial forms with razor-sharp geometric utility.

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Delivers artisanal pedigree, luxury packaging rhythm, and high-contrast grace. Used exclusively for storytelling, category highlights, and hero showcases.
- **Interface Copy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides warm geometric terminals, clear numerals for pricing, and open counters for dense ingredient decks, checkout workflows, and navigation.
- **Accents & Flourishes:** Secondary editorial callouts and vintage tasting notes leverage italicized Playfair Display subsets (`font-style: italic`) for celebratory sensory interludes.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 12-column fluid grid system governs the layout, anchoring product catalogs, tasting profiles, and checkout interfaces within calibrated breathing room.

### Form Factors & Adaptation
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 48px outer margin (`3rem`), 24px gutters (`1.5rem`). Content max-width is constrained to `1280px` to maintain focused visual cohesion.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 32px outer margin (`2rem`), 20px gutters (`1.25rem`). Product grids collapse from 4 columns to 2.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, 20px outer margin (`1.25rem`), 16px gutters (`1rem`). Hero units convert to full-bleed carousels with horizontal snap points.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through soft, chocolate-tinted ambient glows rather than cold industrial dropshadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Base `#FDFBF7` canvas. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Pastry Cards & Selection Tiles):** Layered upon `#F8F4EC` with a low-opacity warm cocoa halo: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(42, 22, 14, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Floating Totes):** Elevated interaction: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(42, 22, 14, 0.09)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Menus, Tasting Drawers):** High-impact structural overlay: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(42, 22, 14, 0.16)`.
- **Surface Accents:** Elements utilize a 1px inner hairline border of `rgba(200, 122, 56, 0.12)` or `rgba(42, 22, 14, 0.04)` to define contours against light surfaces without harsh outlines.

## Shapes

The shape system embraces soft, pill-shaped and rounded geometries reflecting hand-dipped truffles, rolled croissants, and polished confectionery boxes. 

- **Containers & Product Cards:** Use soft expansive radiuses (`rounded-2xl` / `1.5rem` to `rounded-3xl` / `2rem`) to create an inviting, unhurried feel.
- **Buttons, Tags & Input Fields:** Utilize total pill curvature (`rounded-full`) for tactile frictionlessness, evoking polished bonbon lozenges.
- **Imagery Containers:** Utilize soft arch masks or curved aspect ratios to evoke classic French patisserie display vitrines.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep roasted cacao (`#2A160E`) background, milk cream (`#FDFBF7`) text, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `14px 28px`. Hover shifts smoothly to rich caramel (`#C87A38`) with elevation Level 2.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with a 1.5px border in burnished caramel (`#C87A38`), text in dark chocolate (`#2A160E`). Hover applies a warm almond glaze wash (`#EFE7D8`).
- **Accent Action:** Spun copper (`#B87333`) gradient overlay for limited reserve selections and bespoke gift box orders.

### Cards (Confection & Product Showcase)
- **Structure:** Crisp cream background (`#F8F4EC`), 24px internal padding, `rounded-2xl` curvature, anchored by subtle warm cacao elevation Level 1.
- **Imagery:** Aspect ratio 1:1 or 4:5 with soft `rounded-xl` corners and warm milk background fill to emphasize artisan gloss.
- **Meta:** Typographic title in Playfair Display (`title-lg`), price in bold Plus Jakarta Sans (`#C87A38`), accompanied by flavor profile pill tags.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Container:** Warm almond wash (`#EFE7D8`), pill-shaped for single fields or `rounded-xl` for multiline custom cake notes.
- **Borders & States:** 1px border matching `rgba(42, 22, 14, 0.08)`. Focus shifts border to burnished caramel (`#C87A38`) paired with a 3px soft caramel ambient halo (`rgba(200, 122, 56, 0.15)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Radios:** Rounded-full disc in dark chocolate (`#2A160E`) with an inner milk cream core when active.
- **Checkboxes:** Soft `rounded-md` (6px) almond base. Checked state transitions to warm caramel (`#C87A38`) displaying an embroidered cream checkmark.

### Chips & Flavor Badges
- **Tasting Notes:** Mini pill tags (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding, styled in `label-sm` uppercase.
- **Tonal Treatments:** Hazelnut Praline (`#EFE7D8` background, `#2A160E` text), Grand Cru Reserve (`#2A160E` background, `#D4A373` gold text).

### Specialist Component: Flavor Notes & Allergen Matrix
- **Layout:** Horizontal strip of micro-capsule tags with rounded-full geometry, utilizing light cocoa outlines (`rgba(42, 22, 14, 0.1)`) and subtle botanical/cocoa iconography to convey notes (e.g., "Madagascar Bourbon Vanilla", "72% Guayas Cacao", "Gluten-Free").
